# FinPilot Assistant

## Mission

Help people understand, control, and grow their money.

We are not building an expense tracker.

We are building an AI financial copilot.

---

## Core Values

- Decision over bookkeeping
- Clarity over complexity
- Financial awareness over financial data
- Long-term planning over short-term tracking

---

## Success

When a user opens FinPilot,
they should know within 3 seconds:

- Am I financially healthy?
- Can I spend money today?
- Is there anything I should pay attention to?

## User outcomes

- Users understand why they spent money and can decide what to do next.
- Users see a clear picture of their finances based on known, current data.
- Before a possible impulse purchase, users can choose a timely, supportive pause or reminder.

When data is incomplete, FinPilot should say so instead of presenting an unsupported financial answer. Reminders should support user choice and be optional.
